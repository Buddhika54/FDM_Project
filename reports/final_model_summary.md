The production estimator is **tuned XGBoost** (`n_estimators=100`, `max_depth=3`, `learning_rate=0.2`), paired with the Phase 2 `preprocessor.pkl`. Test ROC-AUC is 0.596 — on the same ~0.59 plateau as LR (0.590) and RF (0.594); that is a ranking ceiling from weak numeric signal, not a high-accuracy detector. XGBoost is chosen over RF because the train–test gap is 0.013 (vs RF 0.071) and over LR because recall-first flagging needs a slightly better ranker with a closed gap, while LR remains the calibration reference. The operating threshold is **0.4** (not 0.5): hold-out precision 50.2%, recall 85.8%, flagging about 81% of orders. Use these figures as expected production behaviour; do not advertise accuracy well above 60%.
